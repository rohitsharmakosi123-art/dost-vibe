How it works

Here’s the planned experience for the DOST VIBE Android app.

  1. Buy in-app coins

    Coins are intended for paying for calls in DOST VIBE and cannot be transferred between users. Coin pack prices and call rates are pending confirmation.

  2. Choose a verified partner

    Choose a partner and request a one-to-one voice or video call in the app.

  3. Use coins for the call

    Calls are paid for using your coin balance. Verified partners earn a share of eligible call revenue under separate partner terms.

DOST VIBE is in pre-launch. This website provides information about the planned app.
