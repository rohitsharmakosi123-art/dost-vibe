About DOST VIBE

DOST VIBE is an Android app in development for paid one-to-one voice and video calls in India.

Calls and coins

Users will use nontransferable in-app coins to pay for calls with verified partners.

How partners earn

The planned model gives verified partners a share of eligible call revenue. Partner earnings are separate from the coins users buy for calls. Revenue shares, settlement schedules and verification procedures are pending confirmation.

Launch status

The app is in pre-launch. Public downloads, calls and coin purchases are currently unavailable.
