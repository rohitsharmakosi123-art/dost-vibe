Terms

These pre-launch terms describe the scope of this website and the planned DOST VIBE service. App terms are pending confirmation.

Pre-launch website

This site provides information about DOST VIBE. Account registration, call booking and coin purchases are currently unavailable here.

Paid calls and coins

DOST VIBE plans to offer paid one-to-one voice and video calls in its Android app. Coins are intended for in-app calls and are nontransferable. Pricing, charging rules and any coin expiry rules are pending confirmation.

Partner verification and earnings

The planned service uses verified partners and shares eligible call revenue with them. Verification criteria, payout rules and partner obligations will be covered in separate partner terms.

App terms before launch

Service eligibility, acceptable use, payment terms and procedures for reporting issues will need to be confirmed before the paid service starts.
